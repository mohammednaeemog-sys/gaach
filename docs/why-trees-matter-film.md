## Sound

- Narration: the six script lines, generated with the open-source Kokoro-82M voice model (Apache-2.0), voice "af_heart". A recorded human voice can replace it with a re-mix.
- Music: an original score written for this film (piano, strings and cello), rendered with the FluidR3 GM soundfont (MIT).
- Ambience: the bulbul clip's birdsong, plus a soft breeze and a low city hum.
- The mix is about −15 LUFS, with peaks at −1.5 dBFS. The music ducks under the voice.

## Look

The grade is warm and natural, with greens pulled down slightly toward olive, lifted charcoal shadows and warm highlights. Scene 3 is cooler and flatter. Every shot gets the same fine film grain and a light vignette so the clips match.
